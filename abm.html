ABOUT US

Mapúa University SHS - ABM Society is an organization founded for the Senior High School students who are interested in studying Accountancy, Business, and Management. It provides students with collaborative and comprehensive services. The organization plays a critical role in the development of stakeholder insights as well as the ability to foresee a better future generation.

MISSION

Mapua ABM Society believes in and pledges to serve the university, the business, accounting profession. community innovative contributions to the development of service. In line with the University's mission, our service will primarily focus on applied and phenomenological concerns as we fight for service that is responsive to our stakeholders' changing demands.

VISION

Mapúa ABM Society will be the center of long-term growth, with the utmost knowledge and skill of integrity. Asserts themselves as a dynamo and proponent of methodical and logical thinking for developing a sturdy and entrepreneurial attitude among stakeholders.
