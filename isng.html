ABOUT US

Information Systems NextGen (ISNG) is an esteemed academic organization housed within the School of Information Technology (SOIT) at Mapua University. Our primary mission is to cater to students who have a keen interest in the field of Information Systems, as well as those who wish to delve into the intricate relationships between technology and business processes. We extend our membership to students across various programs within the SOIT, School of Multimedia Sciences (SMS), and the E.T. Yuchengco School of Business and Management (ETYSB) departments. Our organization is committed to fostering an inclusive environment that encourages exploration and growth in the realm of Information Systems.

MISSION

Information Systems Next-Gen of School of Information Technology at Mapua University integrates PEOPLE, TECHNOLOGY, and PROCESS to connect the world.

VISION

Information Systems Next-Gen envisions its members to be futuristic technologists, innovative pioneers, and technological visionaries who use their knowledge and skills to solve problems, make a difference in the world, and create a better future for all.

This vision statement is aligned with the mission of Mapúa University, which is to "empower the youth by providing an education grounded on academic excellence and strength of character." It also reflects the values of Mapúa, which are discipline, excellence, commitment, integrity, and social responsibility.
