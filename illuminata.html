ABOUT US

Illuminata Mapúa specializes in capturing the illuminated essence of life's essential moments, turning ordinary scenes into extraordinary narratives. Our photography celebrates the inherent beauty and significance found in every fleeting instance.

MISSION

IMAP's mission is to promote and celebrate the visual arts while nurturing a creative and supportive community for its members.

VISION

IMAP's vision is to create an inclusive and innovative space for visual artists to develop their skills, exchange ideas, and engage with their communities, guided by principles of sustainability and advocacy for the arts.
