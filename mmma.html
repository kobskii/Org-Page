ABOUT US

The Mapúa Makati Music Association is a non-profit organization that aims to unite Mapúa Makati students through their love for music and encourage musical upskilling and exposure.

MISSION

To help Mapúans explore different genres, learn new techniques, and connect with like-minded individuals who share their passion for music through events, performances, and external exposure support.

VISION

To bring together Mapúans who love and are skilled in music to create exceptional performances and cultivate a secure space for all music enthusiasts.
